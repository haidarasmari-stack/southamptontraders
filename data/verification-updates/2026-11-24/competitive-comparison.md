# Competitive comparison and product scope — 24 November 2026

## Verified public features
- MyBuilder's official homeowner flow lets a visitor post a job, add pictures, hear from interested local tradespeople, review quotes/profile information, then contact interested tradespeople. Its page describes job posting as free and describes post-job reviews. Source: https://www.mybuilder.com/how-it-works
- Checkatrade's current explainer describes up to 12 checks (including identity, address, qualifications where relevant, company and financial checks), ongoing monitoring, and review checks. “Up to” is the platform's wording, not a claim that each member receives every check. Source: https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/expert-advice/checkatrade-how-we-work/
- Checkatrade's support article describes a homeowner option to request multiple quotes and says its matching ranker chooses relevant reviewed trades, with the number varying by job complexity. Source: https://www.checkatrade.com/support/trade/trade-jobs/request-a-quote

## Southampton Traders gap and chosen improvement
Southampton Traders currently has a researched but explicitly non-exhaustive directory, seven launch areas and nine service categories. It publishes no customer reviews or accreditation badges, and its quote/request/report forms are demonstrations that do not send or save submissions. Do not imitate competitor verification, reviews, guarantees, quote matching or lead promises without the real underlying process. The immediate safe improvement is a browser-local shortlist that helps a visitor save and compare listed profiles without an account, sensitive data collection, or pretending that a quote marketplace exists.
