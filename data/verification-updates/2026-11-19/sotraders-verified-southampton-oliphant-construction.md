# Southampton candidate verification: Oliphant Construction

**Decision: eligible.** Oliphant Construction is not listed in the existing builder index by name or official website. The index also contains a separate `REDS BUILDERS LIMITED` profile. The opened REDS official site identifies that business as REDS BUILDERS LIMITED, gives its address as Alchorne Place, Portsmouth, Hampshire P03 5QL, and describes its construction, refurbishment, groundworks and maintenance work. Oliphant Construction instead identifies itself at The Pump House, Archery Road, Southampton, SO19 9GG, and its own page states that Oliphant Developments Ltd is associated with NHBC registration. No evidence reviewed links Oliphant to REDS; these are distinct profiles, not an alias duplication.

The Oliphant official homepage describes it as a building contractor/developer in the Southampton area and lists new builds, loft conversions, extensions, internal/external works, and kitchen and bathroom refurbishments. Its contact details are given on the official website as The Pump House, Archery Road, Southampton, Hampshire, SO19 9GG, telephone 023 8044 1616. The services page provides specific descriptions of new builds, loft conversions, extensions and kitchen/bathroom refurbishments. This supports the local residential builder criterion and these service categories: **New builds** (primary); **Extensions**, **Loft conversions**, **Kitchens & bathrooms**, **Renovations** (secondary; the site expressly describes renovating houses into flats and refurbishments).

Companies House lists OLIPHANT DEVELOPMENTS LIMITED, company number 02071314, as active at The Pump House, Archery Road Woolston, Southampton, Hampshire, SO19 9GG, with SIC 43390 (other building completion and finishing). This status is corroborative, not relied on alone for current trading: the official company website independently presents the building services, Southampton service area, contact details and project-gallery link.
