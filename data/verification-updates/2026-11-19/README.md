# Verification notes for Southampton Traders directory expansions

This folder preserves opened-source notes for additional businesses researched for the directory. Each eligible note records the decision, source URLs and the facts supporting current activity, Southampton locality and explicitly stated residential services. No ratings, reviews, accreditations, credentials or missing contact details were inferred.

## Earlier expansion — 19 November 2026

The first expansion added 17 businesses across Southampton (5), Fareham (1), Winchester (3), Totton (1), Hedge End (3) and Romsey (4). Their evidence notes remain in this folder.

## Southampton target expansion — 19–20 November 2026

A further 41 Southampton businesses passed individual verification: five from the first candidate pass, 18 from postcode-sector research and 18 from completed trade-category checks. Their CSV-ready records and opened sources are preserved in `southampton-target-100/`, `southampton-target-100-phase2/` and `southampton-target-100-phase3/` respectively. Duplicate, ineligible, failed and unfinished candidates were not imported.

That phase brought the repository dataset to 129 records overall and 56 Southampton profiles. Two further Lite batches on 21 November added five eligible Southampton businesses; their notes and candidate outcomes are indexed in `../2026-11-21/README.md`. Current totals are 134 profiles overall and 61 in Southampton, leaving 39 more Southampton profiles to verify to reach the user's target of 100. The earlier trade-category workflow stopped with `creditNotEnough`; no additional credits were purchased, and unfinished checks from that workflow are not treated as evidence.
