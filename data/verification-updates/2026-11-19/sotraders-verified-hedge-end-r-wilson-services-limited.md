# Verification: R WILSON SERVICES LIMITED

**Expected primary area:** Hedge End
**Decision:** Eligible, with service fit limited to Kitchens & bathrooms.

The existing-builder index does not list R WILSON SERVICES LIMITED or R Wilson Services. It does contain a separate REDS BUILDERS LIMITED page. The reviewed official company and service sources do not establish that R Wilson Services is an alias of REDS BUILDERS LIMITED, so the candidate is not rejected as a duplicate on available evidence.

Companies House identifies R WILSON SERVICES LIMITED, company number 04946429, as active and gives its registered office as Unit 1c, Herald Industrial Estate, Herald Road, Hedge End, Southampton, Hampshire, SO30 2JW. The record lists SIC 43220 for plumbing, heat and air-conditioning installation. Its confirmation statement was shown overdue, so Companies House status alone is not treated as proof of trading.

The company's official website provides current trading evidence: it describes domestic plumbing, gas and heating services, gives local coverage including Southampton, and publishes a Hedge End head-office address matching the Companies House address. Its service page expressly offers full bathroom/kitchen installation, including supply and fit and wall and floor tiling. That directly supports the permitted service category “Kitchens & bathrooms.” No extensions, renovations, loft conversions, new builds, brickwork, roofing, plastering or groundworks are claimed in the record.

The official contact page lists telephone 02380 600 960. The official website is https://www.rwilsonservices.co.uk/. The company name, local address, activity and offered installations are supported by primary sources. The record qualifies as a local residential trade business with a permitted service, without relying on third-party directories.
