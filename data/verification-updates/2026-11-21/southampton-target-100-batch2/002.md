# Verification update 002 — Exbury Homes Ltd

## Decision: Eligible

Exbury Homes Ltd is eligible for a Southampton Traders profile. The company’s official Southampton page explicitly offers house extensions and refurbishment in Southampton, and states “Besides new builds” in the same discussion. It invites customers to contact its Southampton builders about new builds, refurbishments and extensions. These statements establish current residential services in the target location. The selected exact allowed service labels are **Extensions** and **New builds**; refurbishment is not substituted with the allowed label “Renovations”. The registered/office addresses found are outside SO14–SO19, but an address in the target postcode is not needed because the official page directly evidences Southampton residential work. The supplied CSV was searched for both “Exbury” and “exburyhomes”; neither matched, so no duplicate was found.

Companies House identifies **EXBURY HOMES LIMITED**, company number **02050666**, as **Active**. Its registered office is 8 Spur Road, Cosham, Portsmouth, Hampshire, PO6 3EB. The official company website separately prints Fairview House, 2a Osborne View Road, Hill Head, Fareham, Hampshire, PO14 3JN, and phone **01329 661840**. Both addresses are retained as evidence only; the profile’s address field uses the official website’s published business contact address.
