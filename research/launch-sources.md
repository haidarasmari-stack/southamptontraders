
- The active project config (`GET config`) reports `analytics.enabled: true`. The Webdev site-settings guide says this injects Umami and Plausible scripts; it also cautions that other platform/CDN analytics may remain even when those scripts are disabled. Sources: `/home/ubuntu/skills/webdev/references/site-settings.md`, https://umami.is/privacy, https://plausible.io/data-policy.
- Umami's policy says its standard analytics do not store visitor IP addresses in analytics records, but process IPs transiently for requests/visitor identification/approximate location; it notes that Umami and infrastructure providers may also retain IPs in operational/security logs. Plausible's data policy says it stores page path/referrer/device/location/engagement metrics, does not store raw IP or full User-Agent, discards most query parameters, and sends requests carrying IP/User-Agent for processing. Provider statements are linked from the site privacy/cookie page; neither third-party policy guarantees the behavior of all Manus or CDN operational logs. These analytics do not receive this app's form values because the app has no analytics event calls or form submission requests.
