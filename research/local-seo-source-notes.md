# Source notes for Southampton Traders local SEO strategy

Research date: 22 November 2026. These are source notes, not the finished strategy.

- Google Search Essentials says SEO helps search engines understand content and users find a site; no particular site is guaranteed indexing or first place. Google says the majority of search listings are found automatically, and other pages' links help discovery. Sitemap submission can help but is not required. Source: [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).
- Google LocalBusiness structured data can describe business facts and may support eligibility for search features, but requires policy compliance and accurate properties. Google advises testing with Rich Results Test and URL Inspection, and pages must be crawlable/indexable. It recommends the most specific LocalBusiness subtype; don't invent address, phone, hours, coordinates, prices, reviews, or credentials. Source: [Google Local Business structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business).
- OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot (used to surface sites in ChatGPT Search) from GPTBot (model-training crawl controls) and says these settings are independent. Opting out of OAI-SearchBot means pages will not appear in ChatGPT search answers, though navigational links may remain. Source: [Overview of OpenAI Crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).
- Perplexity says PerplexityBot is used to surface/link websites in search results; Perplexity-User supports requests initiated by users. Each has separate controls; published IP lists are authoritative for firewall allowances. Source: [Perplexity Crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers).

Pending: official Google Business Profile local visibility and link spam guidance; FAQ/structured-data eligibility; official Bing discoverability guidance; workflow research on Southampton SERP competitor pages.

- Google says eligible Business Profiles require in-person contact with customers; online-only brands/directories and lead-generation companies are examples of ineligible businesses. Southampton Traders should not create its own GBP unless it independently meets the eligibility rules. Source: [Business Profile eligibility and ownership guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en).
- Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity/prominence; complete, accurate information helps relevance, and links/reviews contribute to prominence. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. This is most directly about eligible businesses' own Business Profiles, not a promise about directory organic rankings. Source: [Tips to improve your local ranking on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).
- Google classifies buying/selling links for ranking, excessive reciprocal links, automated link building, low-quality directory links and low-value link-bait content among link-spam examples; paid advertisements/sponsorship links should qualify links appropriately. Source: [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- Google primarily generates snippets from page content and may use a meta description where it is a better summary; it recommends unique, relevant descriptions and may rewrite them. Source: [Control your snippets in search results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).
- Google's Search documentation notes that the FAQ rich-result feature stopped appearing in Google Search from May 7, 2026; any Q&A content for users/voice/AI should be useful visible page text, not added under an implied FAQ rich-result benefit. Source: [FAQ structured-data documentation and change history](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage).
- Microsoft Bing's Webmaster Guidelines state that core SEO practices also support eligibility for Bing/Copilot grounding, and emphasize crawlable internal/external links, canonical sitemaps and accurate freshness signals, clean URL handling, clear HTML and visible verifiable facts. Structured data does not guarantee visibility. Source: [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a).
- Bing IndexNow notifies participating search engines of changed URLs but does not guarantee crawling/indexing. Source: [Bing IndexNow: Get Started](https://www.bing.com/indexnow/getstarted).

The project robots.txt is `User-agent: * / Allow: /`, with the canonical sitemap URL. Therefore OpenAI/Perplexity bots are currently not specifically blocked by the file; no firewall allowlisting is verified. The site's llms.txt accurately describes its current scope but Google says llms.txt is not needed for Search and has no positive or negative visibility/ranking effect. Treat it as optional interoperability documentation, not an SEO lever.
- Google Search's 2026 AI optimization guide says generative features build on core Search ranking/quality systems; pages must be indexed and snippet-eligible, but indexing/serving is not guaranteed. It says no special AI markup, `llms.txt`, or exact wording hacks are needed for Google Search, while crawlable, useful, non-commodity content and clear technical structure remain important. Source: [Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
- Google's AI features guide says there are no extra AI Overviews/AI Mode technical requirements beyond Search eligibility and no special schema; performance is reported in Search Console's Web search type. Source: [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
- Google Search Console Performance reports can show queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position, but results vary by place, time, device and history. Source: [Performance report (Search results)](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en).
- Bing's IndexNow process notifies engines of changed URLs; it does not guarantee crawling/indexing. Source: [IndexNow: Get Started](https://www.bing.com/indexnow/getstarted).
- Live-domain probes were inconsistent: `urllib` direct requests from this runtime received HTTP 403 for multiple public paths; the stateful user browser rendered the actual 135-profile homepage, while the independent competitor-audit agent saw a Hostinger parked-domain page. Treat live search accessibility as unresolved for crawler-facing verification; do not infer that the current application is universally down or universally accessible.
